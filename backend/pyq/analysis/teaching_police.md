## 1. CTET (Central Teacher Eligibility Test)

### 1.1 Exam Pattern Overview

CTET is conducted by CBSE, typically twice a year (July and December). Since 2021, it has been conducted in online (CBT) mode.

#### Paper I (Classes I-V: Primary Stage)

| Subject                          | Questions | Marks | Duration      |
|----------------------------------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy     | 30        | 30    |               |
| Language I (compulsory)          | 30        | 30    |               |
| Language II                      | 30        | 30    |               |
| Mathematics                      | 30        | 30    |               |
| Environmental Studies            | 30        | 30    |               |
| **Total**                        | **150**   | **150** | **2.5 hours** |

#### Paper II (Classes VI-VIII: Upper Primary Stage)

| Subject                          | Questions | Marks | Duration      |
|----------------------------------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Child Development & Pedagogy     | 30        | 30    |               |
| Language I (compulsory)          | 30        | 30    |               |
| Language II                      | 30        | 30    |               |
| Mathematics & Science OR Social Studies | 60  | 60    |               |
| **Total**                        | **150**   | **150** | **2.5 hours** |

- No negative marking
- Qualifying marks: General 60% (90/150), OBC/SC/ST 55% (82/150)
- CTET certificate valid for lifetime (since 2021; earlier it was 7 years)

## 2. UGC NET Paper I

### 2.1 Exam Pattern Overview

UGC NET is conducted by NTA. Paper I is common for all subjects and tests teaching/research aptitude.

| Component      | Details                        |
|----------------|--------------------------------|
| Mode           | Online (CBT)                   |
| Questions      | 50 (Paper I) + 100 (Paper II) |
| Marks          | 100 (Paper I) + 200 (Paper II)|
| Duration       | 3 hours (combined)            |
| Negative Marking | None                        |
| Qualifying     | Based on NTA percentile        |

### 2.2 Paper I Syllabus Units & Weightage

| Unit | Topic                                    | Avg Questions | Weightage |
|------|------------------------------------------|---------------|-----------|
| 1    | Teaching Aptitude                        | 5-6           | 10-12%    |
| 2    | Research Aptitude                        | 5-6           | 10-12%    |
| 3    | Reading Comprehension                    | 5             | 10%       |
| 4    | Communication                            | 4-5           | 8-10%     |
| 5    | Reasoning (including Mathematical)       | 5-6           | 10-12%    |
| 6    | Logical Reasoning                        | 5-6           | 10-12%    |
| 7    | Data Interpretation                      | 5-6           | 10-12%    |
| 8    | Information & Communication Technology   | 4-5           | 8-10%     |
| 9    | People & Environment                     | 4-5           | 8-10%     |
| 10   | Higher Education System                  | 4-5           | 8-10%     |

### 2.3 Most Repeated Topics (2005-2026)

#### Teaching Aptitude - High Frequency

- Characteristics of effective teaching
- Learner-centered vs teacher-centered approaches
- Methods of teaching: Lecture, Discussion, Demonstration, Project method
- Levels of teaching: Memory, Understanding, Reflective
- Bloom's Taxonomy (Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation)
- Evaluation types: Formative, Summative, Diagnostic
- Micro-teaching components
- Classroom management strategies

#### Research Aptitude - High Frequency

- Types of research: Basic, Applied, Action, Descriptive, Experimental
- Research methods: Survey, Case Study, Experimental, Historical
- Steps in research process
- Sampling methods: Probability (Simple Random, Stratified, Cluster, Systematic) and Non-Probability (Convenience, Purposive, Snowball)
- Hypothesis: Types (Null, Alternative, Directional, Non-directional)
- Variables: Independent, Dependent, Intervening, Moderating
- Research Ethics: Plagiarism, Citation, Fabrication
- Thesis/Dissertation writing
- Measures of central tendency and dispersion

#### Communication - High Frequency

- Types: Verbal, Non-verbal, Written, Visual
- Barriers to communication: Physical, Semantic, Psychological, Cultural
- Models: Shannon-Weaver, Berlo's SMCR, Osgood-Schramm
- Classroom communication
- Effective communication characteristics
- Mass communication concepts

#### ICT - High Frequency

- Internet terminology: URL, HTTP, FTP, IP Address, DNS
- E-learning concepts: MOOCs, SWAYAM, NPTEL
- Computer basics: RAM, ROM, Input/Output devices
- MS Office functions
- Cyber security: Virus, Firewall, Encryption
- Social Media in education
- Abbreviations: HTML, XML, PDF, Wi-Fi, LAN, WAN
- Digital India initiatives

#### Higher Education System - High Frequency

- UGC: Role, Functions, Establishment
- NAAC: Accreditation framework
- AICTE, NCTE, NCERT roles
- NEP 2020 key provisions
- Autonomous colleges and deemed universities
- Distance Education: IGNOU, State Open Universities
- National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF)
- RUSA (Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan)
- GER (Gross Enrollment Ratio) in Indian higher education

#### Reasoning & Data Interpretation - High Frequency

- Number Series and Letter Series
- Blood Relations
- Direction and Distance
- Coding-Decoding
- Syllogisms
- Venn Diagrams
- Data sufficiency
- Bar charts, Pie charts, Line graphs interpretation
- Mean, Median, Mode calculations
- Assertion-Reason type questions

### 4.3 State-Specific Differences

#### Bihar STET
- Strong emphasis on language pedagogy in Hindi
- More questions on Inclusive Education than other states
- Bihar state education policies occasionally included
- Paper II has separate Science and Social Science streams

#### MPTET (Madhya Pradesh TET)
- Also called Samvida Shikshak Varg (Grade 1, 2, 3)
- Grade 3 = primary (like Paper I), Grade 2 = middle, Grade 1 = high school
- MP-specific educational schemes may appear
- Strong pedagogy component (NCF 2005)

#### REET (Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers)
- Rajasthan GK & culture questions (5-10 questions) are unique
- Level 1 (Primary) and Level 2 (Upper Primary)
- Rajasthani educational initiatives may be asked
- Hindi literature questions tend to be more detailed

#### HTET (Haryana Teacher Eligibility Test)
- Three levels: PRT, TGT, PGT
- Haryana GK section (5-8 questions)
- Emphasis on Haryana state educational policies
- PGT level includes subject-specific advanced questions

#### OTET (Odisha TET)
- Conducted in Odia medium primarily
- Paper I and Paper II structure similar to CTET
- Odisha state education policy questions
- Focus on tribal education and inclusive practices

#### WB TET (West Bengal TET)
- Two papers: Primary and Upper Primary
- Bengali medium dominant
- West Bengal Board curriculum-aligned questions
- Strong focus on child psychology

#### TN TET (Tamil Nadu TET)
- Paper I and Paper II, Tamil medium dominant
- 3-hour duration (slightly longer than others)
- Tamil Nadu Samacheer Kalvi curriculum-aligned
- Strong emphasis on teaching methodology

#### Karnataka TET (KAR TET)
- Two papers, Kannada medium primary
- Karnataka state curriculum-aligned
- Focus on rural education methodology
- Separate exam for different medium schools

#### AP TET (Andhra Pradesh TET)
- Telugu medium primary
- AP State curriculum-aligned
- Strong pedagogy component
- Separate papers for different subjects at upper primary level
